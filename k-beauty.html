Simple, informed beauty care

K-Beauty is often associated with hydration, layered care, ingredient-focused products and consistent routines. A routine does not need to contain many products to be useful.

What is K-Beauty?

K-Beauty refers broadly to Korean skincare, cosmetics and beauty culture. Korean beauty has developed a strong international following through its focus on skincare, product innovation, ingredients and adaptable routines.

01

Skin-first approach

Many Korean beauty routines place skincare before makeup, with emphasis on cleansing, hydration and maintaining comfortable-looking skin.

02

Ingredient awareness

K-Beauty products commonly highlight specific ingredients and textures, making ingredient literacy useful when comparing products.

03

Flexible routines

A Korean-inspired routine can be simple or layered. The best routine depends on individual skin needs, preferences and product tolerance.

A simple Korean-inspired skincare routine

You do not need a traditional multi-step routine to benefit from K-Beauty principles. Start with the basics and add products only when there is a clear reason to do so.

1

Cleanse

Remove dirt, sunscreen and makeup using a cleanser appropriate for your skin and the products you have used.

2

Hydrate

Use a toner, essence or another lightweight hydrating product if it suits your routine.

3

Treat

Add a serum or targeted cosmetic product when there is a specific skincare goal and the formulation is appropriate for your skin.

4

Moisturize

Finish with a moisturizer suited to your skin type and climate to help maintain comfortable-looking skin.

5

Protect

In the morning, finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen and follow the product directions for application and reapplication.

Keep it practical: More products do not automatically mean better skincare. Introduce new products gradually and stop using products that cause persistent irritation or discomfort.
