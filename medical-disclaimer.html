3. Consult a Qualified Healthcare Professional

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions that could affect your health, particularly if you:

  • Have a diagnosed medical condition or ongoing symptoms.
  • Take prescription medicines or over-the-counter medications.
  • Are considering starting, stopping, or changing a treatment.
  • Are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning a pregnancy.
  • Are considering dietary changes, supplements, or a new exercise program that may affect an existing condition.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read on this website.
